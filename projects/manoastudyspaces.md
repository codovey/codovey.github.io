# The Concept
My group members and I needed an excellent idea for a final project that would stand out in some way and solve a problem some students may have. I had a few ideas in mind before the project began. I took a look around my group members' portfolios and found that <a href="https://tanner-tashiro.github.io/">Tanner</a> already made a conceptual essay for a study location based website. Genius! After a bit of group discussion, we decided on Manoa Study Spaces.
